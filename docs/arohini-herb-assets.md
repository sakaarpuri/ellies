# Arohini ingredient visuals

The gallery uses original AI-generated botanical still lifes.
WebP versions are under `public/images/`. The gallery displays each image in
a 4:3 crop with an HTML caption.

## Prompt set

- `arohini-herb-shatavari.webp`: Photorealistic Shatavari (Asparagus racemosus)
  slender tapered roots and fine green foliage on pale pink limestone. Portrait
  3:4; natural window lighting; botanical subject in upper two thirds; clean
  lower quarter for a caption. No lettering, branding, people or packaging.
- `arohini-herb-ashwagandha.webp`: Photorealistic Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)
  dried slender roots, ovate leaves and berries in papery calyx on pale sage
  stone. Portrait 3:4; natural side light; tactile detail and soft shadows;
  clean lower quarter. No lettering, people, product or packaging.
- `arohini-herb-brahmi.webp`: Photorealistic Brahmi (Bacopa monnieri) creeping
  stems, opposite small succulent obovate leaves and white flowers on pale
  blue-grey limestone. Portrait 3:4; natural morning light; clear lower quarter.
  Not Centella leaves. No lettering, people or packaging.
- `arohini-herb-moringa.webp`: Fresh Moringa oleifera cutting with paired oval
  leaflets and pale green branching stems on cream limestone.
  [Botanical reference](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Moringa_leaves.jpg)
- `arohini-herb-ashoka.webp`: Saraca asoca flower clusters with orange and
  gold blooms, long stamens and glossy lance-shaped leaves on warm limestone.
  [Botanical reference](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Saraca_asoca(flowers).JPG)
- `arohini-herb-guduchi.webp`: Tinospora cordifolia vine with pointed,
  heart-shaped leaves and radiating veins on blue-grey limestone.
  [Botanical reference](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tinospora_cordifolia.jpg)
- `arohini-herb-amalaki.webp`: Phyllanthus emblica fruits with faint vertical
  ridges and narrow leaflets on sage limestone.
  [Botanical reference](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fruits_of_Phyllanthus_emblica_L._-_Flickr_-_lalithamba.jpg)

The four additions were generated on 18 November 2026 using the linked
photographs as species references and the existing still lifes as style
references. The common prompt specifies a natural-light macro photograph
in a centred 4:3 composition, with no lettering, props or packaging.
